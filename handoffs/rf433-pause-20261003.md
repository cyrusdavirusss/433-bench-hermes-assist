- **433, ESP + CC1101**, 95 passes over 432.0-434.6 MHz: best jump between passes **+3.0 dB**,

  carrier was found and the WATCH phase never opened. **The operator never confirmed a press
  inside that window**, so this is a measured window, not a verdict about the device.
- **315, YARD Stick One**, 6 min on 315.000 MHz with nothing keyed: floor **-107.0 dBm**,
  ambient tops **-90.0 dBm = +17 dB over floor**, hits 26 / 6500 bytes, `detected=False`.
  Read this as the bench's 315 noise profile: **+17 dB over floor at 315 is NOT a press** under
  the bench's own +20 dB decisive bar, and hits/bytes accumulate on noise alone. A press will
  need to clear +20 dB before it means anything.
- Rig identity unchanged: `fw=1.0.0`, `cc1101_version=0x14`, `layout=vspi-documented`,
